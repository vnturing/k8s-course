Kubernetes on Incus

A crash course for one Intel laptop, 16 GB of RAM, and four virtual machines.

You don’t need a rack of servers. You’ll launch four Debian VMs with Incus, treat them as separate machines, and build a real multi-node k3s cluster. Each lesson is short, ends with a small win, and asks you to recall rather than reread.
